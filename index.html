Customs Service

The Customs Service collects duties under the Customs Code of 2008, keeps the customs posts at the frontier crossings with the Republican Gendarmerie, and runs the Talas Customs Clearance Terminal for bonded freight.

Notices

23 October 2026
Kum-Shagyl crossing closed for the winter

The customs post at Kum-Shagyl closed on 22 October and will reopen in spring. Traffic for Kazakhstan should use Chu Gate or the Talas North Terminal.

Frontier posts directorate

12 October 2026
Electronic declarations: system maintenance

The electronic declaration system will be unavailable on 1 November from 01:00 to 05:00. Declarations may be lodged on paper at the Terminal during that time.

30 September 2026
Tankers must show their register entry

From 1 October fuel tankers are cleared only on showing their entry in the Gendarmerie's register of tankers.
