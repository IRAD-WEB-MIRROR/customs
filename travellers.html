Travellers

Travellers may bring personal belongings into the Zone free of duty. The following limits apply to each adult.

Tobacco200 cigarettes or 250 g of tobacco
Alcohol2 litres
Other goodsUp to the value of USD 500
CashSums over USD 10,000 or the equivalent in som must be declared

Restricted goods

  • Firearms and ammunition: only with a permit from the Ministry of Internal Security.
  • Horses and livestock: veterinary certificate and the fees at the crossing.
  • Ore and minerals: not permitted without papers from a licensed works.
  • Narcotics: prohibited.

Travellers crossing the line from the tribal side need a travel permit registered by the Gendarmerie.
