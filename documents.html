Documents and legal acts

Orders, regulations and reports of the Customs Service, given for information. The Service's acts are made under the Customs Code of 2008 and do not change it. Where the text on this site differs from the signed copy, the signed copy prevails.

Order · 16 October 2026 · No. 108-o

On the seasonal closure of the Kum-Shagyl customs post

In force

Closes the post at Kum-Shagyl for the winter from 22 October 2026; the staff are reassigned to Chu Gate and Talas North Terminal until the post reopens in spring.

Order · 9 October 2026 · No. 105-o

On maintenance of the electronic declaration system on 1 November 2026

In force

The system is stopped on 1 November from 01:00 to 05:00; during that time declarations are accepted on paper at the Terminal.

Order · 28 September 2026 · No. 102-o

On the clearance of fuel tankers

In force

From 1 October 2026 fuel tankers are cleared at the posts and the Terminal only on showing their entry in the Gendarmerie's register of tankers.

Annual report · 12 February 2026 · AR-2025

Annual report of the Customs Service for 2025

Published

4.31 billion som transferred to the budget; about 43,000 containers cleared at the Terminal; 389 cases of undeclared or wrongly declared goods, 61 of them ore and concentrates; shared hours with the Uzbek posts from March.

Order · 26 February 2025 · No. 19-o

On the working hours of the customs posts at Kyzyl-Tuu and Dostuk

In force

Applies the shared inspection hours provided by the February 2025 memorandum with Uzbekistan from 3 March 2025.

Full text ›

Annual report · 14 February 2025 · AR-2024

Annual report of the Customs Service for 2024

Published

Revenue transferred to the budget, the work of the Terminal and the posts, seizures and the training of staff in 2024.

Order · 20 January 2025 · No. 9-o

Instruction on the weighing and sampling of ore and mineral loads

In force

From 1 February 2025 every load of ore, concentrate or other mineral leaving the Zone is weighed and sampled.

Full text ›

Order · 3 October 2022 · No. 77-o

Code of professional conduct of officers of the Customs Service

In force

Rules of conduct for officers: courtesy to travellers and carriers, refusal of gifts, declaration of any personal interest in a consignment, and the regular rotation of officers between posts.

Order · 21 March 2020 · No. 38-o

On the work of the customs posts under the quarantine regime

No longer in force since 1 September 2022

Restricted contact between officers and drivers at the posts and closed the paper declaration window at the Terminal for the period of the quarantine regime.

Order · 13 February 2017 · No. 31-o

Procedure for the reception of citizens and the consideration of written appeals

In force as amended by Order No. 54-o of 9 June 2025

Sets the days of personal reception by the leadership, the registration of appeals and the 30-day period for a reply.

Order · 2 March 2015 · No. 47-o

On the introduction of electronic customs declarations at the Talas Customs Clearance Terminal

In force

Introduces the lodging of declarations in electronic form at the Terminal and the X-ray inspection of containers, and sets the period during which paper declarations are still accepted.

Statute · 20 November 2008 · 2008/41

Statute of the Customs Service of the Irad Special Economic Zone

In force

The founding statute of the Service, approved by executive directive of the Governor: status, tasks, leadership and seat of the Service.
