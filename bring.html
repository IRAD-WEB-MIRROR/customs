What can I bring in?

Say where you are coming from, how long you were away, who is travelling with you and what is in the bag. The page tells you for each item whether it is allowed, allowed with a declaration, allowed with duty, needs a permit from another body or is forbidden, with the duty in som, the form and the window.

The allowances change on 1 January and 1 July each year. Both the allowances in force and those from 1 January 2027 are shown. The general limits for each adult are on the Travellers page.
