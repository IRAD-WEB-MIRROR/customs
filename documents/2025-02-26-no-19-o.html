Order · 26 February 2025 · No. 19-o

On the working hours of the customs posts at Kyzyl-Tuu and Dostuk

In force

1. In application of the memorandum on trade and border coordination between the Irad Special Economic Zone and Uzbekistan of February 2025, from 3 March 2025 the customs posts at Kyzyl-Tuu and Dostuk open and close at the hours of the Uzbek posts opposite.

2. The Frontier Posts Directorate agrees the hours with the Uzbek side through the heads of the posts and publishes any change on the website of the Service not later than three days before it takes effect, except in urgent cases.

3. A vehicle that has passed the Iradi post before closing is cleared in full. Vehicles are not left between the posts after closing.

4. The rosters of the posts at Kyzyl-Tuu and Dostuk are set accordingly. Control over this order is entrusted to the deputy director A. Sultanbekov.
