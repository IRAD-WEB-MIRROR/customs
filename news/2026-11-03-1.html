“What can I bring in?”: allowances for travellers now checked on the website

A page on this website now gives travellers the rules for the goods they carry: the route, the length of the stay abroad, the family and the contents of the bag give, for each item, whether it is allowed, allowed with a declaration, allowed with duty, needs a permit from another body or is forbidden, with the duty in som, the form and the window. The page applies the allowances in force and shows those from 1 January 2027 under Order 111-o of 27 October 2026.

A ten-question test on the same page lets travellers check their knowledge before the journey. The answers of the page are for information; binding answers are given only in writing (Order 31-o, para 2.4). What can I bring in?
