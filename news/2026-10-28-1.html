28 October 2026 · No. 38

New inspectors complete their course and go to the posts

On 27 October 31 newly recruited inspectors completed a six-week course at the central office. The course covered the Customs Code, the duty bands and regimes, the electronic declaration system, the inspection of vehicles and the weighing and sampling of ore.

Deputy director A. Sultanbekov handed the new inspectors their service certificates. They will serve at Chu Gate, Talas North Terminal, Kyzyl-Tuu and the Terminal, where the winter roster begins on 1 November.

Recruitment for the posts continues; open vacancies are listed on the vacancies page.

Press service of the Customs Service
