Results of the work of the Service in 2025

In 2025 the Customs Service transferred 4.31 billion som to the budget of the Zone in customs duties, transit charges and the export duty on antimony concentrate, 6% more than in 2024.

The Terminal cleared about 43,000 containers. Officers found 389 cases of goods not declared or wrongly declared; 61 of them concerned ore and mineral concentrates, among them the seizure of 46 tonnes of stibnite at Talas North Terminal in August.

For 2026 the Director set the tasks of shorter waiting at the northern crossings, the training of new inspectors for the posts and the full application of the rules on the clearance of fuel tankers. The annual report is published in the documents section.
