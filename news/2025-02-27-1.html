Kyzyl-Tuu and Dostuk to keep the hours of the Uzbek posts

Under the memorandum on trade and border coordination with Uzbekistan, signed this month at the offices of the Lieutenant Governor in Talas, from 3 March the customs posts at Kyzyl-Tuu and Dostuk open and close at the same hours as the Uzbek posts opposite.

Lorries will no longer be held overnight between the two posts. The hours are given on the crossings page and will change together with the Uzbek side.

Patrols on the frontier remain coordinated, not joint.
