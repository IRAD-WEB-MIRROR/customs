Deputy director for the frontier posts and the Terminal appointed

Anvar Sultanbekov has been appointed deputy director of the Customs Service responsible for the frontier posts and the Talas Customs Clearance Terminal.

On 5 September Director U. Yakupov introduced him to the heads of the posts at a meeting at the Terminal. Among the first tasks named were a review of the duty rosters at the northern posts and the regular rotation of staff between posts.

Press service of the Customs Service
