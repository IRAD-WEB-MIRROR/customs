Work of the Service during the New Year holidays

On the public holidays the central office in Talas does not receive visitors. The Terminal, Chu Gate, Talas North Terminal, Nurzhan rail border station and Suzak work round the clock as usual; Kyzyl-Tuu and Dostuk keep the hours of the Uzbek posts.

The electronic declarations help desk answers from 08:00 to 20:00 on all days of the holidays. The Service congratulates participants of foreign trade and all residents of the Zone on the New Year.

Press service of the Customs Service
