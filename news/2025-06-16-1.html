Ten years of electronic declarations at the Terminal

In 2015 X-ray scanners and electronic declarations were introduced at the Talas Customs Clearance Terminal. In ten years more than 350,000 declarations have been lodged there in electronic form.

Today almost every declaration at the Terminal is lodged electronically. Paper declarations are accepted at the local crossings only.

On 13 June Director U. Yakupov presented certificates of honour to 17 members of staff who have worked with the system since its first day.

Press service of the Customs Service
