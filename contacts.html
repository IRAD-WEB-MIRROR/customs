Written appeals

Written appeals may be sent by post to the address above, by e-mail to info@customs.iradgov.org, on the e-Irad form OG-1, or handed in at the records office (window 1, ground floor). An appeal must give the name and postal or e-mail address of the sender. It is registered on the day it is received and answered within 30 days; where a check is needed, the period may be extended once by 30 days, and the sender is informed. Anonymous appeals are not answered, but information on smuggling is checked whether it is signed or not.

How to get here

The Terminal is in the Talas Free Economic Trade Zone, west of Semiz. By road: take the Trans-Talas Highway west from Talas, pass Semiz and turn at the sign for the Zone; visitors use Gate 2 and the car park in front of the Administration Building. By public transport: marshrutkas and buses for Semiz leave from the station square in Talas (about 25 minutes); change at Semiz for the shift buses of the Zone or walk about 15 minutes from the last stop. Visitors are admitted with a passport or residence card. Photography at the Terminal is not permitted.

Office hours

Central office: Monday to Friday, 09:00–18:00, break 13:00–14:00; closed on Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays. The Terminal clears goods round the clock. The hours of each crossing are on the crossings page. The electronic declarations help desk answers every day from 08:00 to 20:00. Appointments for reception are made at the Reception of the Director; please bring a passport or residence card.
