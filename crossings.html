Crossings

Customs posts are kept at the crossings held by the Zone, alongside the Border Demarcation Brigade of the Republican Gendarmerie. Goods may be declared only at these posts.

Hours

Under the memorandum signed with Uzbekistan in February 2025, the customs posts at Kyzyl-Tuu and Dostuk keep the same hours as the Uzbek posts opposite. The Kum-Shagyl crossing opens in summer only. Delays at the Kazakh posts are announced in the notices when the Kazakh side reports them.

Tribal crossings

The Customs Service has no posts at the crossings on the tribal side of the line. Goods brought in through them have not been declared and are liable to seizure in the Zone.
